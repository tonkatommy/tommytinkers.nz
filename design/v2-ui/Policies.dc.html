I don't sell your information, I don't run ads, and I don't use tracking or analytics scripts. This policy follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

If you order something to be posted to someone else, I collect their name and address from you. I use them only to deliver that order, and your shipping email tells them it came through you.

Who else sees it

I use a few trusted services to run the site. Each one only gets what it needs for its job:

  • Stripe processes payments. You enter your card on Stripe's page, not mine.
  • Resend sends emails such as receipts, password resets and shipping updates.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to the site, protects it from attacks, and runs the Turnstile check on sign-up to keep bots out.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • NZ Post gets your name and address on the parcel.

Some of these companies store information outside New Zealand, mainly in the United States. I only use services that protect personal information to a standard comparable to New Zealand law. I'll also share information if the law requires it, for example with Inland Revenue or the police with a proper legal request.

Where it's kept and how long

The site and its database run on a server I manage in New Zealand. Access is limited to me, and backups are encrypted and kept for 30 days.

  • Orders and receipts: 7 years, because New Zealand tax law requires it.
  • Artwork uploads: kept with your order for 7 years, so I can reprint or reorder for you. Ask me any time and I'll delete them sooner.
  • Accounts and Shed posts: until you delete your account.
  • Enquiries: 2 years, then deleted unless we're working together.
  • Back-in-stock requests: until I've sent the email.
  • News emails: until you unsubscribe. I keep a note that you did, so you're never added again by mistake.
  • Data downloads: the file is deleted after 7 days.
  • Server logs: 30 days.

Cookies and storage

The site uses a small number of cookies that it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, one that remembers your light or dark theme, and the ones Cloudflare uses to protect the site. Your motion and effects setting is saved in your own browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment page.

Your account and emails

You can change your profile, email and password in your account settings at any time. Receipts and shipping emails always go out because they're part of your order. Shed notifications you can turn off in settings. News about new stickers only comes if you tick the box at checkout or turn it on in settings, and every non-essential email has an unsubscribe link.

If you delete your account, your profile, posts, pins and badges go for good. Your order records stay for tax purposes, but they're no longer linked to a login.

Your rights

You can ask me what personal information I hold about you, and ask me to correct it. Email hello@tommytinkers.nz and I'll reply within 20 working days, usually much sooner. It's free. With a Shed account, you can also download a copy of everything I hold about you from your account settings.

If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can fix it. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

If something goes wrong

If there's a privacy breach that could seriously harm you, I'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as I can, as the law requires.

Children

Anyone can browse and buy. You need to be 13 or older to make a Shed account, or have a parent or guardian's OK.

Changes

If I change how I handle your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top. If it's a big change, I'll email account holders first.

Prices and payment

All prices are in New Zealand dollars. I'm not registered for GST, so there's no GST to add or claim. You pay through Stripe Checkout, and I never see your full card details. Your order is confirmed once Stripe confirms payment, and you'll get a receipt by email.

I check every price on the server when you check out. If a price on the site is clearly wrong, I'll contact you before I make anything and you can choose to pay the right price or get a full refund.

Discounts

Discounts and codes work as shown in your cart, and only one code can be used per order unless the discount says otherwise. I can change or end a discount at any time, but never on an order you've already paid for.

Personalised and made-to-order items

Most of what I sell is printed to order with the details you give me, so please check your spelling, names, numbers and colours before you pay. I print exactly what you enter.

If you spot a mistake, contact me straight away with your order number. If I haven't printed it yet, I'll fix it for free. Once it's printed, a reprint is charged as a new order unless the mistake was mine.

If you upload artwork, you're confirming you own it or have permission to use it. I won't print anything I think is offensive, harmful or someone else's trademark or copyright. If that happens, I'll tell you and refund that item in full.

For custom sheets, I email you a proof to approve before I print anything. Ask for as many changes as you need. Once you approve it, I print exactly that, so check it carefully.

I usually print and post made-to-order items within 5 working days. If something will take longer, I'll email you. If I'm away, a notice at the top of the site says when I'm back and when orders will be posted.

Shipping

I post anywhere in New Zealand with NZ Post, and I only ship to New Zealand addresses. Posting is free anywhere in New Zealand. You can also collect your order free at a race day I'm going to, or from Helensville at a time we arrange by email. You'll get a tracking link by email when your parcel is on its way.

Your order is my responsibility until it reaches you. If it's lost or damaged in the post, tell me within 14 days of the expected delivery date and I'll reprint it or refund you.

Returns and refunds

Your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act always apply. If something's faulty, damaged, or not what you ordered or what I described, I'll repair, reprint or refund it. Just email me a photo and your order number.

Because personalised and made-to-order items are made just for you, I can't accept change-of-mind returns on them.

For items that aren't personalised, I'm happy to take a change-of-mind return within 14 days of delivery if it's unused and in its original packaging. You pay the return postage, and I refund the item price once it's back with me.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with through Stripe. They can take 5 to 10 working days to reach your account.

Buying for a club or business

If you're buying for a business or club, such as a set of grid stickers for a race meeting, you agree the Consumer Guarantees Act doesn't apply to that purchase, as the Act allows. I'll still put right anything that's faulty or wrong.

Club orders are quoted. The quote sets the price, what's included, how long it's valid and when you pay. You can pay an invoice by card through Stripe, or by bank transfer with the invoice number as the reference.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself and your email up to date. You're responsible for what happens on your account. After 5 wrong passwords in a row, sign-in locks for 15 minutes to keep your account safe.

You can delete your account at any time in your settings. I may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or the house rules.

The Shed

The Shed is free to join, and anyone can read the Pinboard. To keep it friendly:

  • Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats or personal attacks.
  • Only post things you have the right to share, and no one else's personal details.
  • No spam, scams or selling without asking me first.
  • Nothing illegal, explicit or dangerous.

The house rules page has the detail. A new account's first 3 posts are checked by me before they go live. I can hide or remove any post, and lock threads, at any time.

You own what you post. By posting it, you let me display it on tommytinkers.nz for as long as it's up. You can delete your posts at any time.

Codebench work

The Codebench build sheet gives you a ballpark price, not a quote. Any project we agree on is covered by a written quote or agreement, which sets the price, scope and timeline. Where that agreement differs from these terms, it wins.

My content

The designs, photos, text and code on this site belong to me unless I say otherwise. Please don't copy or resell them without asking. Your artwork stays yours. You give me permission to use it only to make your order.

If something goes wrong

I do my best to keep the site running and accurate, but I can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes. Where the law allows, my responsibility for any order is limited to what you paid for it. This doesn't limit any rights you have under New Zealand consumer law.

Changes and the law
